A federated learning study that predicts heart disease from clinical biomarkers without centralising patient data.

## Details

- Uses a dataset of 918 subjects with 11 clinical features, including age, sex, chest pain type, resting blood pressure, cholesterol, fasting blood sugar, resting ECG, maximum heart rate, exercise-induced angina, oldpeak and ST slope.
- Trains the predictive model in a federated setting so that sensitive medical records stay local to each client while still contributing to a shared global model.
