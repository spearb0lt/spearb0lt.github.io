LitigatAI is a Streamlit-based AI platform built for Indian advocates, litigation interns and legal researchers. It bundles 10 integrated tools that automate the most time-consuming parts of legal practice, from extracting dates out of case files to drafting counter-arguments against an opponent's pleadings.

## Stack

- Multi-page Streamlit web app.
- Primary LLM: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.
- Fast routing LLM: Groq Llama 3.3 70B.
- Fallback LLM: an OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
