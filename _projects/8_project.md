Research from my time at the CMATER Lab, Jadavpur University, on identifying lung cancer from medical images. This work was accepted at IEEE ISACC 2025.

## What it does

- Builds a custom architecture based on transfer learning and soft-attention mechanisms that can detect and classify lung cancer from both CT scans and histopathological images.
- Experiments with attention, PCA, colour-channel splitting analysis and image partitioning to improve classification across multi-source medical images.
- Proposes a method that generalises across datasets from different sources, a common challenge in medical imaging.
