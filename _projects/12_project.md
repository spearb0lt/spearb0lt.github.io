A local web app (also usable as a Chrome extension) that lets you tailor every section of your LaTeX resume for a specific job and compile straight to a submission-ready PDF, using the exact same fonts and layout as your original resume.

## Features

- Toggle any entry, bullet or skill on or off with checkboxes across all six sections (Education, Skills, Experience, Projects, Publications, Achievements).
- Bring back commented-out content, add new entries with rich-text formatting, and drag to reorder.
- Optionally let an LLM pre-select the most relevant content from a pasted job description.
- Keeps your original `base.tex` untouched; all output is written to a separate folder.
