Hi, I am Shubhro Dev, a final-year Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at the [Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT)](https://www.rgipt.ac.in/), an Institute of National Importance built along the lines of IITs. I work at the intersection of large language models, multimodal deep learning, federated learning and time-series analysis, and I care most about building models that are not just accurate but also verifiable and privacy preserving.

I am currently a Technical Research Intern with the Global DAI R (SDE-IN) team at [Siemens](https://www.siemens.com/), researching the validation and verification of outputs generated by LLMs. Before this I spent time as a research assistant and intern at labs including [VANET Lab (IIT Jodhpur)](https://vanets-iitj.github.io/), [VCA Lab (IIT BHU Varanasi)](https://www.iitbhu.ac.in/dept/cse/people/skscse) and [CMATER Lab (Jadavpur University)](https://www.cmaterju.in/home), and as a data science and machine learning intern at [Core-AI Solutions](https://core-ai.in/) and [Adani Power](https://www.adanipower.com/operational-power-plants/dahanu-maharashtra). Along the way I have worked on predictive maintenance, medical imaging, and multimodal knowledge retrieval.

My work has led to publications in medical imaging and privacy-preserving predictive maintenance, and I placed at [Global Rank 10](https://www.facebook.com/codingcompetitions/hacker-cup/2025/round-2/scoreboard?track=AI_CLOSED_TRACK&source=facebook) in the Meta Hacker Cup 2025 (AI Track). When I am away from research, you will usually find me playing [chess](/hobbies/), building software tools, or gaming.

Feel free to explore my [publications](/publications/), [projects](/projects/) and [CV](/cv/), or reach out over any of the links below.
